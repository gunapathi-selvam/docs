Galaxy Spiral

Turn on your camera and move your hands in front of the laptop. The swarm follows your palm, rolls with your wrist, and re-forms into a new shape for every number of fingers you hold up.

  • Move — the cloud tracks your palm around the screen
  • Rotate — tilt your wrist to roll it, sweep sideways to spin it
  • Resize — push toward the lens or pull away
  • Shape — hold up 0–5 fingers
  • Grab — pinch thumb and index to crush it into your hand

Video never leaves your machine — tracking runs entirely in the browser. The hand model is fetched from a CDN on first use.
